Roomgist — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Roomgist is a browser extension that lets you chat in real time with other people viewing the same web page (same hostname and path).

What we collect

  • Username — a random display name generated on first use and stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local). You do not provide your real name or email.
  • Chat messages — text you send is transmitted to our Cloudflare Worker backend and stored temporarily in a Durable Object room keyed by the page URL (hostname + path). Up to 200 recent messages per room are kept; inactive rooms are deleted after 30 days.
  • Page URL — the extension reads location.hostname and location.pathname to determine which chat room to join. The full browser URL is not sent to our servers unless it appears in a message you type.

What we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, email, or phone number.
  • We do not track your browsing history beyond the current page path needed to join the correct chat room.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

How data is used

Data is used solely to deliver real-time chat: showing message history, presence (who is online), and typing indicators to other users in the same room.

Third-party services

Chat traffic is processed on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects. Cloudflare's privacy policy applies to infrastructure processing.

Data retention

Messages are limited to the 200 most recent per room. Rooms with no activity for 30 days are automatically deleted.

Permissions

  • storage — save your username and whether the chat panel is open.
  • host access (<all_urls>) — inject a chat button on pages you visit so you can open the panel on any site.

Children

Roomgist is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on the project repository.
